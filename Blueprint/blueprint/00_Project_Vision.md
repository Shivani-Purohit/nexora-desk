**Status:** Draft

# Vision

Nexora Desk is an AI-powered omnichannel helpdesk platform that enables businesses to manage customer conversations, support tickets, and team collaboration from a single modern workspace.

The platform focuses on speed, simplicity, and intelligent automation to improve both customer experience and support team productivity.

---

# Mission

To build a modern, scalable, and user-friendly helpdesk platform that combines multiple communication channels with artificial intelligence, allowing support teams to resolve customer issues faster and more efficiently.

---

# Problem Statement

Many businesses use multiple applications to manage customer communication. This creates fragmented conversations, duplicate work, and slower response times.

Nexora Desk solves this by providing a single platform where support agents can handle conversations, manage tickets, collaborate with teammates, and receive AI-powered assistance.

---

# MVP Goals

The first version of Nexora Desk will include:

- User Authentication
- Organization Management
- Customer Management
- Ticket Management
- WhatsApp Integration
- Telegram Integration
- AI Assistant
- Dashboard
- Reports
- Knowledge Base
- Notifications

# Success Criteria

The MVP will be considered successful when:

- Tickets can be created and managed.
- WhatsApp and Telegram conversations generate tickets.
- AI can summarize conversations.
- AI suggests replies to agents.
- Dashboard displays real-time statistics.
- Reports are generated successfully.
- User roles and permissions work correctly.

# Conclusion

Nexora Desk aims to become a modern AI-powered support platform that provides businesses with a fast, intelligent, and scalable way to manage customer support from a single interface.
